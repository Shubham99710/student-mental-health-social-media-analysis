**AI & Social Media Impact on Student Mental Health**

An interactive Tableau dashboard examining the relationships between social media usage, AI tool adoption, daily screen time, physical activity, sleep patterns, and overall student mental/physical health metrics across different demographics.

**Overview**

This project analyzes student well-being by correlating daily digital habits with mental and physical health scores. Using data visualization, the dashboard highlights key patterns in how social media and AI consumption influence student lifestyles.

**Key Visualizations & Insights**

Summary KPI Cards: High-level metrics tracking Average Mental Health Score (Avg MHS), Physical Health Score (Avg PHS), Daily Social Media Hours (Avg D.S.M.H.), Daily AI Tool Usage Hours (Avg D.AI.T.U.H.), Sleep Hours, and Physical Activity Hours.

**Mental & Physical Health Distribution:** Overview of health score distributions across student demographics.

**Social Media vs. Mental Health:** Scatter/trend analysis mapping daily social media hours against mental health scores.

**Demographic Health Matrix:** Detailed crosstab evaluating health indicators across gender and academic groups.

**Gender Distribution of Social Media Time:** Comparative breakdown of daily social media consumption by gender.
